A generated project's `sdlc` plugin gains `/sdlc:sdlc-status` and a session-start hook that lists each change in `intent/` that is not done, with its stage and who acts next. The UI stacks' `AGENTS.md` tell the agent to look at a changed page with the Playwright MCP server, and the plugin's eval workflow also runs nightly.

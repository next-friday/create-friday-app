Generated projects get a `docs/` folder for documentation of the system as it is now, organized by Diátaxis, with architecture decision records in `docs/adr/`. `intent/README.md` sizes the documents to the change: a small change needs none, a medium change needs `plan.md`, and only a large change needs `intent.md` and `spec.md`. Before merging, what stays true moves into `docs/`.

A generated project now holds the `sdlc` plugin instead of copies of its skills. `plugins/sdlc/claude-code/` and `plugins/sdlc/cursor/` hold the plugin, and `.claude-plugin/marketplace.json` lists it as `sdlc` in the marketplace `next-friday-plugins`. The commands are `/sdlc:sdlc-mode`, `/sdlc:sdlc-help`, and the new `/sdlc:setup-sdlc`, which chooses the model for each role and reports which steps of `SETUP.md` are done. A team replaces a default policy with its own `.agents/skills/<area>-policy/SKILL.md`, which wins over the plugin's default. The skills, the `verifier` subagent, the eval cases in `.agents/skill-evals/`, `scripts/run-agent-evals.ts`, the `evals:run` script, and the workflow `agent-evals.yml` no longer ship; each stack's `AGENTS.md` now states how the `verifier` subagent starts the app, `SETUP.md`, `intent/README.md`, and `REVIEW.md` name the plugin, and `CONTRIBUTING.md` loses its eval step. `scripts/check-plan.ts`, `scripts/check-bands.ts`, and `scripts/measure-loop.ts` stay, because `pnpm verify`, the pre-push hook, and CI call them.

A project generated earlier keeps working as it is. To move one, copy `plugins/sdlc/` and `.claude-plugin/marketplace.json` from a new project of the same stack, add `extraKnownMarketplaces` with the entry `next-friday-plugins` (source `directory`, path `.`) and `enabledPlugins` with `sdlc@next-friday-plugins: true` to `.claude/settings.json`, then delete `.agents/skills/` (except any policy of your own), `.claude/skills/`, `.cursor/skills/`, `.agents/skill-evals/`, `.agents/agents.json`, the `verifier.md` files under `.agents/agents/`, `.claude/agents/`, and `.cursor/agents/`, `scripts/run-agent-evals.ts`, `.github/workflows/agent-evals.yml`, and the `evals:run` script. Replace `/sdlc` with `/sdlc:sdlc-mode` in the two workflows `spec-on-intent-merge.yml` and `control-band.yml`, and pass `--plugin-dir plugins/sdlc/claude-code` in their `claude_args`. For Cursor, copy `.cursor/hooks/load-project-plugins.sh` and its `workspaceOpen` entry in `.cursor/hooks.json` from a new project.

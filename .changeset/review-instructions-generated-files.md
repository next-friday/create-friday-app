The review instructions in `.agents/review.md` and `REVIEW.md` name the generated files each stack has, so a review skips them: `pnpm-lock.yaml` in every stack, and the Payload types and migrations in the `payload` and `turborepo` stacks. For a change classed as higher risk, the product owner consults a tech lead before accepting the spec. Each stack's `AGENTS.md` carries the rules on issues, on what belongs in the repository, and on sourced requirements.
